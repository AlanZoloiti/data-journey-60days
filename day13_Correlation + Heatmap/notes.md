# Day 13 — Notes (Correlation)

## 📊 Correlation

Correlation = relationship between variables  

---

## 📏 Range

-1 → strong negative  
0 → no correlation  
+1 → strong positive  

---

## 🔥 Interpretation

high correlation → strong relationship  
low correlation → weak relationship  

---

## ⚠️ Important

correlation ≠ causation  

---

## 📊 Heatmap

visual representation of correlation  

---

## 💡 annot=True

shows numbers on heatmap  

---

## 🎯 How to Analyze

1. look at correlation with target (revenue)  
2. ignore diagonal (1.0)  
3. focus on strongest values  

---

## 🔥 Feature Thinking

strong correlation → candidate feature  
weak correlation → low priority  

---

## ❗ Common Mistake

weak correlation ≠ influence  

---

## 🚀 Insight

analysis → decision making  

---

## 💡 Rule

find signal → ignore noise
