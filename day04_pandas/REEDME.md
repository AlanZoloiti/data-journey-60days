# Day 4 — Pandas (GroupBy & Analytics)

## 📊 Tasks

* Calculated revenue by country
* Analyzed revenue by country and customer segment
* Computed average check per user

## 🧠 Approach

* Prepared and cleaned data
* Used `groupby` for aggregation
* Applied multiple grouping levels
* Converted results into tabular format using `reset_index`

## 📈 Result

* Identified revenue distribution across countries
* Analyzed contribution of different customer segments
* Calculated average spending behavior per user

## 💡 Insight

Revenue is unevenly distributed across segments and countries, with high-value users contributing the most.
