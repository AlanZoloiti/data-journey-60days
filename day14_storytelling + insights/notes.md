## 📏 Metrics

ARPU = revenue per user  
LTV = lifetime value  

---

## 🔥 Important

ARPU ↑ ≠ LTV ↑  

---

## 🧠 Retention

retention ↓ → users leave faster  
→ LTV ↓  

---

## 🚀 Thinking

---

## 💡 Rule

optimize system, not one metric  

---
