# Day 14 — Data Storytelling & Product Thinking

## 🎯 Goal
Learn how to interpret data and communicate insights in a business context.

---

## 🧠 What I Learned

- Data storytelling = explaining data in business terms  
- Observation → Insight → Action framework  
- Difference between short-term and long-term metrics  
- Trade-offs in product decisions  
- ARPU vs LTV  

---

## 🔥 Key Concepts

### Observation → Insight → Action

- Observation → what we see in data  
- Insight → what it means  
- Action → what to do  

---

### Trade-offs

- Increasing ads → higher short-term revenue  
- But → worse UX → lower retention  
- Trade-off between monetization and user experience  

---

### ARPU vs LTV

- ARPU → short-term revenue per user  
- LTV → total revenue from a user over time  

Key idea:
- ARPU can grow while LTV decreases  

---

## 📊 Example Insight

We observe that increasing ads improves ARPU but reduces retention.

This suggests that users generate more revenue in the short term,
but churn faster, reducing overall LTV.

---

## 🚀 Business Thinking

- Do not optimize only for short-term revenue  
- Balance monetization and retention  
- Think in terms of long-term value  

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

- Data without interpretation is useless  
- Always connect metrics to business impact  
- Focus on long-term growth, not just short-term gains
